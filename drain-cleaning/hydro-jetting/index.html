What hydro-jetting does

A drain machine punches a hole through a clog. Hydro-jetting sends high-pressure water through the line to scour the entire pipe wall, removing grease, soap scale, sludge and small roots, so the line stays clear much longer.

When it's the right call

  • Drains that keep clogging after they've been snaked
  • Kitchen lines with heavy grease buildup
  • Main lines with root intrusion (after a camera check)
  • Rentals and businesses with heavy use

Camera first

We usually look inside the line with a sewer camera before jetting, so we know the pipe is sound and can show you the result afterward.

Questions we hear a lot

Is hydro-jetting safe for my pipes?

For pipes in good condition, yes. That's why we inspect with a camera first; if a pipe is damaged, we'll recommend a repair instead.

Does hydro-jetting use chemicals?

No. It uses water.
