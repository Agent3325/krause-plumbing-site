Why inspect with a camera?

Guessing at a drain problem is expensive. A camera shows the exact cause and location, whether it's roots, a sag, a crack or buildup, so you pay for the right fix.

Good times to get one

  • Repeat clogs or slow drains throughout the house
  • Before buying an older home
  • After clearing a main line, to check for damage
  • Sewage smells or wet spots in the yard

What you get

We walk you through the footage and explain your options in plain language.

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need a camera inspection for every clog?

No. Most simple clogs don't need one. We recommend it for repeat problems or main-line issues.

Can you find where the problem is?

Yes. The camera shows how far down the line the problem is, which helps plan any repair.
