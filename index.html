Don't wait for a backup

5 signs your drain needs a plumber

A slow drain rarely fixes itself. Catching it early is almost always cheaper than cleaning up after a backup.

01

Slow draining

Water sits in the sink or tub and takes minutes to go down.

02

Gurgling

Drains or toilets bubble when another fixture runs.

03

Sewer smell

Odor coming up from drains, the basement or the yard.

04

Several clogs at once

More than one slow drain usually means the main line.

05

Water where it shouldn't be

Around a floor drain, cleanout or toilet base.

Questions we hear a lot

How much does drain cleaning cost?

The price depends on the drain and the line, and bigger jobs like main sewer lines or hydro-jetting depend on what we find. Call or book online and we'll talk through your situation.

Should I use a chemical drain cleaner first?

We'd skip it. Harsh chemicals can damage older pipes and rarely clear the whole clog. They also make the job more hazardous once the line is opened.
