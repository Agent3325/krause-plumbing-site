What to do right away

  1. Shut off the water. Turn the main shut-off valve clockwise until it stops. It's usually where the water line enters the house.
  2. Turn off the water heater if the leak is near it or you've shut off the main.
  3. Stop using drains if sewage is backing up.
  4. Move valuables and take photos for insurance.
  5. Call us: (336) 816-8381.

Questions we hear a lot

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Burst pipes, sewage backups, overflowing toilets that won't stop, leaks spreading through ceilings or floors, and no water to the house.

Where is my main water shut-off?

Usually where the water line enters the house: in a basement, crawl space, utility room or near the water heater. On a well, you can also turn off the pump's breaker.
