Prevent frozen pipes

  • Keep the heat at 55°F or higher, even when you're away
  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls
  • Let a faucet drip on the coldest nights
  • Disconnect hoses and protect outdoor spigots
  • Insulate pipes in crawl spaces, garages and attics

If a pipe freezes

  1. Open the faucet it feeds.
  2. Warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer or space heater. Never use an open flame.
  3. Can't find it, or it's cracked? Shut off the main and call us.

If a pipe bursts

Shut off the main water valve right away, turn off the water heater, and call (336) 816-8381.

Questions we hear a lot

How do I know if a pipe is frozen?

Little or no water from a faucet on a cold morning, frost on an exposed pipe, or a bulge in a pipe are common signs.

Can I thaw a pipe myself?

You can gently warm an exposed pipe with a hair dryer while the faucet is open. Never use an open flame. If you can't find the frozen section or the pipe is cracked, shut off the water and call us.
