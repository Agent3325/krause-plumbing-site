Accessibility

Effective October 2, 2026

Our commitment

Krause Plumbing LLC wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we do

  • Every page has a skip link, headings in order and visible keyboard focus.
  • Forms have labels and messages that screen readers announce.
  • Text and buttons use readable contrast and scale with your browser settings.
  • Photos have text descriptions where they carry meaning.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have trouble using any part of the site, tell us and we'll help you another way, such as taking your request by phone. Contact andy.krauseplumbing@gmail.com or call (336) 816-8381. We try to respond within 2 business days.
