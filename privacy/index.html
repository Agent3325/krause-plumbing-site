Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Krause Plumbing LLC ("Krause Plumbing", "we", "us") handles personal information on this website and when you contact us. We are a plumber based at 186 Shady Grove Ln, North Carolina.

Where we operate

Krause Plumbing provides plumbing services only in North Carolina, in Davie County, Charlotte and Concord. This website is not intended to offer services in California or the European Economic Area. We do not knowingly market to people there.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, phone number, town, the service you need, preferred time and the details you type into our request form, plus anything you tell us by phone, text or email.
  • Information collected automatically: your IP address and basic device and browser information in our hosting server logs. If you accept analytics and advertising cookies, those tools also collect pages viewed, device, approximate location and button taps.

Please don't send payment card numbers, government IDs or other sensitive information through the site. We do not collect precise location, health or financial account details.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, schedule and perform plumbing work, and send invoices.
  • To run, secure and improve the website.
  • With your consent, to measure and improve our advertising.
  • To meet legal, tax, licensing and warranty requirements.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information for money. We share it with service providers who help us run the business, such as website hosting, the form service that delivers your request, job-management and accounting software, and, if you accept cookies, analytics and advertising providers (Google and Meta). They may use it only to provide their services to us under their terms.

Consent for analytics and advertising

We load analytics and advertising tools (Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta Pixel) only if you accept them in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the page.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out of analytics and advertising cookies. Because there is no common standard for "Do Not Track," we handle it the same way as a Global Privacy Control signal.

Calls, texts and email

By sending a request you agree that we may call or text you about it. We don't send marketing texts without your separate opt-in, and you can reply STOP at any time. If we use call tracking that records calls, we will say so at the start of the call. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe link.

Your rights if you live in California

California residents may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and share, to ask us to delete or correct it, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell personal information for money. If we use advertising cookies that share information with Google or Meta, we treat that as "sharing," and you can opt out through our cookie banner or by sending a Global Privacy Control signal. To make a request, email andy.krauseplumbing@gmail.com or call (336) 816-8381. We will confirm your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. We may need to verify your identity. You may use an authorized agent.

Your rights if you live in the EU or UK

You may ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict your personal information, to receive a copy in a portable format, to object to certain uses, and to withdraw consent at any time. Our legal bases are: to provide the service you requested (contract), our legitimate interest in running and securing the business, legal obligations, and your consent for analytics and advertising cookies. We keep unconverted service requests for 12 months and customer records as long as the law and warranty needs require. Your data may be processed in the United States by our service providers under standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards. You may complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us at andy.krauseplumbing@gmail.com.
